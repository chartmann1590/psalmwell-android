Child Safety Standards

Psalmwell: Daily Bible Verse (com.hartmannstudios.psalmwell) by Hartmann Studios. Effective October 30, 2026.

Our standard

Psalmwell prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind. This includes any content or behavior that sexualizes minors, grooming, sextortion, trafficking, and any attempt to contact or solicit a minor for sexual purposes. Psalmwell is not directed at children, and accounts are only for people aged 13 and older.

How we prevent and act on CSAE

  • Limited features: reflections are short, text-only and public. There are no private messages, no image or video uploads, and no way to share contact details or links, so there is no private channel between users.
  • Moderation: every reflection is checked automatically on the device and again on our server, including for child-safety terms. Content that matches is blocked before it is published.
  • In-app reporting: every reflection has Report reflection and Report user options, with a "Child safety concern" reason. Anything reported for child safety is hidden right away and reviewed by a person.
  • Blocking: users can block anyone, so they never see that person's content again.
  • Enforcement: we remove CSAE content and permanently ban the accounts involved.
  • Reporting to authorities: we report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) through the CyberTipline, and to the relevant authorities, as the law requires.

Report a concern

In the app, open the menu on a reflection and choose Report reflection or Report user, then choose "Child safety concern". You can also email our child safety contact, Charles Hartmann, at me@charleshartmann.com. We act on child safety reports as our top priority. If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

Compliance

Psalmwell follows applicable child safety laws and Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy. Our designated point of contact for CSAM and child safety is Charles Hartmann (me@charleshartmann.com). This contact can speak to our CSAM prevention practices and how we comply.
