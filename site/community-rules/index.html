Community Rules

Applies to Reflections in Psalmwell: Daily Bible Verse. Version 1, October 30, 2026.

Reflections is a place to share, in a few sentences, what the day's verse means to you. Everyone is welcome, whatever their faith or questions. To keep it kind and safe, everyone who posts agrees to these rules first.

Do

  • Be kind, honest and respectful, including when you disagree.
  • Share your own thoughts, prayers and questions.
  • Report anything that breaks these rules.

Don't post

  • Harassment, bullying, insults or personal attacks.
  • Hate speech, or attacks on anyone because of race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or any other protected characteristic.
  • Threats, encouragement of violence, or content that promotes self-harm or suicide.
  • Sexual content or nudity, or anything that sexualizes or endangers children. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material. We report it to NCMEC and the authorities.
  • Spam, advertising, scams, links, or personal contact details such as phone numbers or email addresses, whether yours or anyone else's.
  • Impersonation of other people or organizations, or anything illegal.

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account.

How moderation works

  • Before posting: every reflection is checked automatically on your phone, then again on our server. Reflections that look like they break the rules are not published.
  • Reporting: open the menu on any reflection to report the reflection or report the user. Reports are private, and the person reported is not told who reported them.
  • Blocking: choose Block user from the same menu, and you won't see that person's reflections again. They aren't notified. You can unblock people in Settings > Blocked people.
  • Review: reports are reviewed by a person, usually within 24 hours. A reflection reported for child safety, threats or sexual content is hidden right away while it is reviewed. So is any reflection reported by several people.
  • Action: content that breaks the rules is removed. Accounts that break the rules can be suspended or permanently banned.

Appeals

If you think we made a mistake removing a reflection or suspending your account, email me@charleshartmann.com with the subject "Psalmwell appeal" and include the email address on your account. You can also do this from Settings > Contact & appeals in the app. A person will review your appeal, usually within 7 days.
