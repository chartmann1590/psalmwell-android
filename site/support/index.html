Support & Appeals

Contact

Email me@charleshartmann.com. We usually reply within 2 business days. Psalmwell is made by Hartmann Studios (Charles Hartmann).

Appeal a moderation decision

Email us with the subject "Psalmwell appeal" and the email address on your account. Tell us which reflection or decision you're asking about. A person will review it, usually within 7 days. See the community rules.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. The verse of the day, the Psalms, saved verses and your journal all work without one. An account is only needed to post, report or block in Reflections.

How do I report or block someone?

Open the menu on one of their reflections and choose Report reflection, Report user or Block user. Manage the people you've blocked in Settings > Blocked people.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings > Delete account and my reflections. Or use the web page.

Which Bible translation is this?

The World English Bible (WEB), a public-domain modern English translation.
