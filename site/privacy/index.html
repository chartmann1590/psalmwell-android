Privacy Policy

Psalmwell: Daily Bible Verse (Android package com.hartmannstudios.psalmwell). Effective October 30, 2026.

This policy explains what the Psalmwell: Daily Bible Verse app ("Psalmwell") collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it. Psalmwell is developed by Hartmann Studios, the developer name of Charles Hartmann, who is responsible for your data. Contact: me@charleshartmann.com.

In short: you can read the verse of the day, all 150 Psalms, your saved verses and your journal without an account, and none of that leaves your phone. An account is only needed to post, report or block in Reflections. The app shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob, and it uses Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes.

1. Data that stays on your phone

Your saved verses, private journal entries, reminder time, text size and the record that you accepted the community rules are stored only on your device. We never receive them. If you turn on your phone's own Android backup, Android may include this app data in that backup, encrypted and controlled by your Google account. Daily reminders are scheduled on your device. No server sends them.

2. Data we collect

DataWhenWhy
Email address and passwordWhen you create an accountSign-in, password reset and account security. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication, and we never see them.
Display name and user IDWhen you create an accountYour display name is shown publicly next to your reflections. The ID links your account to your reflections, blocked list and reports.
Reflections you postWhen you postShown publicly to all Psalmwell users under that day's verse. Each one is checked by automated moderation before it is published.

Crash logs and diagnostics: device model, OS version, app version, the stack trace and a Firebase installation IDWhen the app crashes or hits an errorTo find and fix bugs, through Firebase Crashlytics.
Advertising data: advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, device information and ad interactionsWhile a banner ad is shownTo serve, measure and limit ads, and to prevent ad fraud. This is collected by Google AdMob (see section 3).

Psalmwell does not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or health data.

3. Who we share data with

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Crashlytics) stores account, reflection, report and crash data for us as a service provider. See the Firebase privacy information.
  • Cloudflare runs our moderation API (psalmwell-api), which checks reflections and processes reports and deletions as a service provider.
  • Google AdMob receives the advertising data listed above to show banner ads. Where the law requires it, for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Psalmwell asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalized. You can change your choice anytime in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses data from apps.
  • Other Psalmwell users can see your display name and the reflections you post.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect people from harm. For example, we report child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the authorities.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles ourselves.

4. Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Firebase encrypts stored data at rest. Access to moderation tools is limited to the developer.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account data, reflections and your blocked list are kept until you delete them or your account.
  • Reflections removed by moderation are kept for up to 1 year so decisions can be reviewed and appealed. After that they are deleted.
  • Reports are kept for up to 1 year for safety reasons. When the person who filed a report deletes their account, their ID is removed from it.
  • Rate-limit records are deleted after a few days. Crashlytics keeps crash data for 90 days.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete a single reflection in the app from its menu. To delete your account and all of its data, open Settings > Delete account and my reflections in the app, or go to psalmwell.web.app/delete-account. This permanently deletes your sign-in account, profile, display name, all of your reflections and your blocked list. If you can't sign in, email me@charleshartmann.com from the address on your account, and we will delete it within 30 days. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.

7. Children

Psalmwell is meant for people aged 13 and older, and Reflections is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it. See our child safety standards.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to or limit some processing. Email me@charleshartmann.com and we will respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
