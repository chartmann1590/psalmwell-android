Delete your Psalmwell account

This page deletes your Psalmwell: Daily Bible Verse account (by Hartmann Studios) and everything linked to it:

  • your sign-in account (email and password)
  • your display name and profile
  • every reflection you've posted
  • your blocked list

Reports you filed about others are kept for up to 1 year for safety reasons, with your account ID removed. Your journal and saved verses are stored only on your phone. Uninstall the app to remove them.

You can also delete your account in the app: Settings > Delete account and my reflections.
