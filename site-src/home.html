What's inside

  • Verse of the day. A new verse from the Psalms every day, with a link to read the whole Psalm and an optional daily reminder.
  • All 150 Psalms. Read offline, chapter to chapter, with adjustable text size and light and dark themes. No account needed.
  • Saved verses and a private journal. Keep the verses that speak to you and write a few lines about them. Both stay on your phone.
  • Reflections (optional). Read what others share about the day's verse. To post, you create a free account and agree to the community rules. Every reflection is moderated, and you can report or block anyone.

Free, with a small banner ad

Psalmwell is free. It shows one banner ad at the bottom of the screen and never shows full-screen ads. It asks for your consent where the law requires it, and it doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

The text

Psalmwell uses the World English Bible (WEB), a modern English translation that is in the public domain.
